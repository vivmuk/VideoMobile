Every model here is a real Venice video model. The console only shows you the settings the model you picked actually accepts, so nothing you choose can be rejected for being unsupported.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Pick your starting point: image → video, text → video, or video → video.
  2. Describe the shot: subject, action, camera move, light.
  3. Choose model, shape, length and quality. Defaults are already sensible.
  4. Press GENERATE to see the exact Venice price, then press again to confirm.
  5. Leave the screen if you want. The server keeps the job running and the clip lands in HISTORY.

THE THREE MODES

IMAGE → VIDEO

The default, and the one most people want. You supply one photo and the model animates it. You keep control of the person, product, or place because the first frame is yours.

Best when the look matters more than the story.

TEXT → VIDEO

No photo. The model invents everything from your description. Great for ideas, moods, and scenes you cannot photograph.

Write camera direction and lighting, not just the subject.

VIDEO → VIDEO

Start from a clip you already have. Restyle it, apply motion from it, or upscale it to a sharper resolution.

Upscale models use an ENHANCE factor instead of a quality setting.

WRITING A BETTER PROMPT

  • Name one subject and one action. Two competing actions usually produce neither.
  • Say what the camera does: slow push in, locked off, handheld follow, wide orbit.
  • Say what the light does: low sun, overcast, single hard key, neon spill.
  • Use the ADVANCED negative prompt for what you keep getting and do not want.
  • Change one thing per retry, so you can tell what actually improved.
  • With a starting image, describe the motion — not the things already visible in the frame.

PRIVACY AND COST
